## English

### 1. Information We Collect
**Jelly Pop: Slime Merge** does not require user registration or login and does not operate a personal user-data server.

- **Local Game Data:** Stage progress, stars, coin balance, unlocked slimes, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard device storage (`SharedPreferences`).
- **Advertising (Google AdMob):** This app uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction metrics to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
- **In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing):** Purchases are processed directly by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment account details.

### 2. Children's Privacy
This game is intended for a general audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

### 3. Third-Party Privacy Policies
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

### 4. Data Deletion
You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
